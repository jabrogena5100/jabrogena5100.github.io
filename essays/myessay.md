*Playing around with my iPad*

As one of the first Gen Z to ever own an iPad, I can recall riding my mini-scooter in the front yard of my dad's porch. One day, little me was handed this device and so I played each day on it. Sometimes I even wondered how something portable could be so fun. Sooner or later, I decided to study Computer Engineering and am now on my junior year. Despite my over-usage of the tablet, I really liked numbers and so I decided to combine both of my interests to seek a living in it. 

## Getting things handed to me all the time

I would say I was a spoiled child when I was younger in the way that I had more than my parents when they were growing up. However, that also made me grateful because the people around me made me say that I should do something good as well. I wasn't technical but my memory was my most valuable asset. However, memorizing things weren't always the solution. I also needed to understand the theory and the different methods to solve problems. 

## In the context of engineering

After graduating high school, I felt that I had learned a lot but also so little. Because of this, I had to decided to major in computer engineering because I felt I would grow more. I would be able to solve complex math problems as well as programming problems. In the end, I became satisfied with my decision. I feel that I'm not only growing technically in terms of doing hands-on problems but also I'm learning how to work with other people as well.
