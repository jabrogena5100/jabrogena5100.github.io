**Starting with the iPad**

## Starting with the iPad

As one of the first people in Gen Z to ever own an iPad, I can recall riding my mini-scooter in the front yard of my dad's porch. One day, little me was handed this device and so I played each day on it. Sometimes I even wondered how something portable could be so fun. Sooner or later, I decided to study Computer Engineering because I wanted to learn how devices are built and operated. As long as it had a screen and adjusts according to how you wanted it the computer has stuck with me ever since its inception. In school, I was moderate at math so I put two and two together and said that my job should be with computers.

## The three skills that I want to develop

On a technical note, I do wish to develop my software engineering skills a bit further. I want to experience working with a team on web development. To do this, I will have to further develop my skills in TypeScript, HTML, CSS, and NextJS to make well-made websites. Another thing that I want to develop through software engineering is being good at data science so I can visualize and make predictions about data. Outside of my software engineering class, I also am taking time to learn AI/ML. The third thing that I want to experience is using my software engineering skills to make ideas come true. This can mean working at a startup company where people like our products and apps.

## In the context of engineering

Before college, I felt I wasn't technical at all, but my memory was my most valuable asset because I had knowledge but nothing to show for it. Then again, memorizing things wasn't always the solution as I progressed through mye education. I also needed to understand the theory and the different methods to solve problems. After graduating high school, I felt that I had learned a lot but also so little. Because of this, I had decided to major in computer engineering because I felt I would grow more. I would be able to solve complex math problems as well as programming problems. In the end, I became satisfied with my decision. I feel that I'm not only growing technically in terms of doing hands-on problems but also learning how to work with other people as well.
